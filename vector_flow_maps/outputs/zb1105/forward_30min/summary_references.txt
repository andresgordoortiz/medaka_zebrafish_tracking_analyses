zb1105 -- reference frames used in flow-map analysis
================================================================
Voxel size:            1.250 um isotropic
Frame interval:        120 s (= 2.00 min)
Temporal window:       30 min (= 15 frames) starting at each landmark
Velocity lag:          1 frames (= 2.00 min)
Smoothing window:      1 frames (= 2.00 min)
Spatial bin:           30 um,  min n/bin = 10

Anchored to START OF INTERNALIZATION (3 landmarks, absolute frames):
  1 h before internalisation          (t = 0, window 0-14)
  Start of internalisation            (t = 30, window 30-44)
  1 h after internalisation           (t = 60, window 60-74)

Outputs in this folder:
  04a_flow_30min_after_internalization_zb1105.pdf   (3 internalisation-anchored panels)
  04c_majority_movement_zb1105.pdf                   (majority move-type strip per landmark)
  flow_fields.csv                (raw bin-level flow data, all panels)
  panel_metadata.csv             (per-panel frame window and landmark)
  panel_majority_movement.csv    (per-panel majority move_type)
  summary_references.txt         (this file)
